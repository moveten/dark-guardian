Using the attached image as reference: the LEFT figure is "Azazel, the Archduke of Hell" (a huge demon boss) and the RIGHT figure is a warrior hero used ONLY as a strict style and proportion reference (chibi, thick clean dark outlines, crisp cel shading, saturated colors, game-sprite look).
Create a NEW character: "Baby Azazel", a cute little pet version of the left demon. Keep his identity: curved black horns, bat-like dragon wings with glowing lava veins, charcoal armor plates with gold trim and glowing orange cracks, ember-orange glowing eyes, a tail that ends in a small flame.
